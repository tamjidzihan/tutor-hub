1. PLATFORM OVERVIEW & ROLE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TutorHub is an authenticated-first tuition matching, direct messaging, and peer
learning community platform in Bangladesh.

The platform supports three distinct roles:
1. STUDENT: Posts tuition requirements, searches tutors, sends direct messages,
   reviews tutors with star ratings, and tracks matches on the dashboard.
2. TUTOR: Builds a verified profile, browses and applies to tuition jobs,
   messages student job posters, tracks proposals, and engages in discussions.
3. ADMIN: Moderates tuition jobs, verifies tutor profiles, audits users, and
   monitors platform analytics.

* Note: The legacy PARENT role has been retired and migrated to STUDENT.


================================================================================
2. STUDENT WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

A. HOW A STUDENT POSTS A TUITION JOB / REQUIREMENT
--------------------------------------------------
1. Navigation:
   - On the Tuition Job Board (/job-board) or Home (/), click "Post a Job (Free)".
   - Or navigate directly to: /appoint-a-tutor (or /post-job).

2. Filling Requirement Details:
   - Student info: Student Name, Class Level (e.g. Class 9), Curriculum (Bangla
     Medium, English Version, English Medium, Edexcel, Cambridge, etc.), and
     Subjects to learn (e.g. Higher Math, Physics).
   - Location: Division/City (e.g. Dhaka, Chittagong) and Area (e.g. Mirpur,
     Dhanmondi, Uttara).
   - Logistics & Budget: Number of days per week, preferred tutoring time,
     monthly budget / honorarium in BDT (৳), and preferred tutor gender (Male,
     Female, or Any).

3. Instant Processing:
   - When submitted, the backend saves the requirement to the database.
   - An intelligent matching engine calculates compatibility scores against
     verified tutor profiles in real-time.
   - The job is published to the public Tuition Job Board (/job-board) so
     verified tutors can discover and apply.
   - The student is redirected to their Student Dashboard (/dashboard) where
     they can monitor matched tutors and incoming applications.


B. HOW A STUDENT HIRES A TUTOR
------------------------------
Students have three options to hire a tutor:

Option 1: From Automated AI Matches (Fastest)
1. Open the Student Dashboard (/dashboard).
2. Look at "Your Tuition Requirements" -> "Tutor Matches".
3. Review each matched tutor's match score (e.g., 95% Match), university
   (e.g., BUET, DU, DMC), department, and specific matching reasons.
4. Click "Select Tutor" to confirm the appointment, or click "View Profile"
   to inspect full qualifications first.

Option 2: From Received Job Applications
1. Tutors browsing the Job Board submit proposals with their expected monthly
   salary and a cover message.
2. The student views applications on their dashboard or job details page.
3. The student can click "Message" to negotiate terms with the applicant, then
   click "Select Tutor" to approve.

Option 3: Direct Search & Discovery
1. Visit "Find Tutors" (/find-tutor).
2. Filter tutors by subjects, university, district, tuition type (Home, Online,
   Group), and Min / Max Monthly Budget (৳).
3. Click a tutor's card to view their profile (/hub/tutor-details/:tutorId).
4. Review their educational background, ratings, and Gemini AI review summaries.
5. Click the prominent "Message Tutor" button to discuss schedules and finalize
   the hiring agreement.


C. HOW A STUDENT TEXTS A TUTOR
------------------------------
Method 1 — From Tutor Profile:
1. On any tutor's profile (/hub/tutor-details/:tutorId), click "Message Tutor".
2. The system automatically creates or opens the direct conversation inside the
   dashboard messages center (/dashboard/messages?tutor=TT-T-XXXXXX).
3. The student can type their message and press Enter or click Send.

Method 2 — From the Floating Chat Widget:
1. On any page, click the floating 💬 chat bubble fixed at the bottom-right corner.
2. Select an existing conversation or search for a tutor by name.
3. Chat in real-time without leaving the current page.

Method 3 — From the Dashboard Messages Center:
1. Navigate to /dashboard/messages from the dashboard sidebar.
2. Select any active conversation from the left sidebar list.
3. View message history and send new messages with real-time live updates.

3. TUTOR WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

A. HOW A TUTOR DISCOVERS AND APPLIES TO TUITION JOBS
----------------------------------------------------
1. Go to "Tuition Jobs" (/job-board).
2. Filter listings by:
   - City & Area
   - Class Level
   - Subject
   - Tuition Type (Home Tutoring, Online, Group)
   - Preferred Tutor Gender
3. Click on a job card to open the complete specification (/job-board/:jobId).
4. Review weekly schedule, subjects, curriculum, and offered honorarium (৳).
5. Click "Apply for this Tuition", enter expected salary (৳) and a personalized
   cover message, then click "Submit Proposal".
6. Track application status (Pending -> Shortlisted -> Selected) on the Tutor
   Dashboard (/dashboard).


B. HOW A TUTOR TEXTS A STUDENT OR JOB POSTER
--------------------------------------------
Method 1 — Direct from Job Details:
1. When viewing any tuition job on /job-board/:jobId, the tutor sees the job
   details and poster information.
2. In the bottom action bar, next to "Apply", click the "Message Poster" button.
3. This opens a direct conversation with the student who posted that job at
   /dashboard/messages?user={student_user_id}.
4. Discuss tutoring format, timing, curriculum requirements, or honorarium.

Method 2 — From Applications or Student Replies:
1. When a student replies to an application or sends an inquiry, the tutor receives
   an in-app notification and an unread badge on their bottom-right floating chat.
2. Clicking the floating chat popup or opening /dashboard/messages lets the tutor
   reply immediately.


================================================================================
4. REAL-TIME DIRECT MESSAGING & FLOATING CHAT SYSTEM
================================================================================

1. Floating Chat Bubble (Always Accessible):
   - Fixed to the bottom-right viewport (bottom: 24px, right: 24px) on all pages.
   - Shows live unread count badge (e.g. 💬 3).
   - Smooth slide-up + scale animation when opened directly above the button.
   - Allows switching between Conversation List and Active Chat.
   - Supports instant search, typing, Enter-to-send, and 3.5s real-time message polling.
   - Automatically hides on /dashboard/messages to avoid duplicate UI.

2. Full Dashboard Messages Center (/dashboard/messages):
   - Integrated into the authenticated user dashboard sidebar.
   - Left Panel: Searchable conversation list, participant avatars, names,
     role badges (STUDENT / TUTOR), last message snippet, timestamps, unread pills.
   - Right Panel: Full-height chat stream, outgoing vs incoming message bubbles,
     timestamps, read receipts (Check / CheckCheck), and auto-scroll.
   - Automatically marks conversations as read upon opening.
